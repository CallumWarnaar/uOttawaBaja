Competitions

Where the car gets tested for real

Every design decision gets judged twice: once by industry engineers, and once by the terrain.

2026–2027 season

This season's schedule

Two events: an early-season exhibition race to shake the car down, and the SAE International competition we build toward all year.

Sponsor branding travels with the team to every event — on the vehicle, the paddock banner and team apparel.

  1. Oct 2–4, 2026

    OktoBajaFest

    Seven Springs · Colton, New York

    The largest exhibition race for Baja vehicles in North America, with roughly 30 vehicles from 15 universities. An early chance to run the car in race conditions, refine drivetrain and suspension set-up, collect performance data and give newer members competition experience ahead of the SAE event.

  2. May 20–23, 2027

    Baja SAE Williamsport Target event

    Williamsport, Pennsylvania

    The team's target SAE International competition for 2027, subject to the SAE registration lottery. Four days of static judging, dynamic events and the four-hour endurance race — our principal benchmark against the leading Baja programs in North America.

How it works

What happens at a Baja SAE event

Every team runs the same 14 hp Kohler CH440 engine and meets the same safety rules, so results come down to design, weight, reliability and execution.

Static & business

Technical inspection, design judging, a cost report and a sales presentation, defended in front of industry judges.

Dynamic events

Acceleration, hill climb or traction, manoeuvrability and suspension events under demanding off-road conditions.

Endurance

A four-hour wheel-to-wheel race where reliability and pit-crew coordination matter as much as outright speed.
