Who we are

Built by students. Proven on the dirt.

Rough Rider Racing (RRR) is one of the Faculty of Engineering's competitive design teams. Every season we design, manufacture, test and race a single-seat all-terrain vehicle that has to survive jumps, rocks, mud and a four-hour endurance race.

All design, analysis, fabrication and testing is done by students from mechanical, electrical, software and computer engineering, and other programs, with guidance from faculty and alumni.

About the team
How we work

Four subteams, one car

The team is split into technical subteams that own each system of the vehicle, backed by an administration group that keeps the program funded and on the road.

Chassis

Frame, roll cage, ergonomics and mounting points — stiffness, safety, weight and serviceability.

Suspension

Geometry, springs and dampers, and tuning ride height, handling and durability over rough terrain.

Drivetrain

CVT tuning, gearbox design, gearing, shafts, hubs and bearings for efficiency and reliability.

Electrical

Wiring harness, kill switches, sensor network and data acquisition for safe, reliable operation.
