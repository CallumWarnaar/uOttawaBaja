Ten systems. One car.

Apart from the rules-specified engine, this single-seat off-road car is designed, analysed, built and tested by students. Scroll to take it apart, or tap any point on the car.

Chassis subteam

Chassis & roll cage

A steel space frame with an integrated roll cage, designed for stiffness, driver safety, low weight and easy access to every serviceable part.

  • Material4130 steel
  • Tubing1-1/4 in OD × 0.065 in wall
  • Wheelbase70 in
  • Track width58 in

Suspension subteam

Front suspension

Double-wishbone independent front suspension with King 2.0 air shocks, tuned for ground clearance, wheel travel and stability over jumps, rocks and ruts.

  • TypeDouble wishbone
  • Travel12 in
  • ShocksKing PR2008 2.0 air · 8 in travel
  • Ride height14 in

Suspension subteam

Steering

A power-assisted steering system built for precise, low-speed manoeuvring on tight courses, where the car has to thread cones, logs and hairpins.

  • TypePower steering
  • Turning radius8 ft
  • Ackermann90%

Drivetrain subteam

Brakes

Hydraulic disc brakes at all four wheels on two independent circuits, able to lock all four wheels, as the rules require.

  • Type4-wheel disc
  • Circuits2

Chassis subteam

Cockpit & ergonomics

The driver's seating position, controls and visibility are designed around the drivers, all inside the protection of the roll cage and behind a firewall.

  • SeatCustom foam inserts
  • Harness5-point
  • Egress timeUnder 5 s

Electrical subteam

Electrical & data

Safety shutdown circuits and kill switches, driver controls and wiring packaged to stay reliable through mud and water. A sensor network and data logging are in development.

  • Kill switches2 · interior + exterior
  • SensorsIn development
  • LoggingIn development

Rules-specified

Engine

Every team runs the same engine specified by the Baja SAE rules: currently a Kohler CH440, rated at 14 hp stock and restricted to 10 hp for competition. The power is fixed, so the gains have to come from the rest of the car.

  • ModelKohler CH440
  • Power10 hp (restricted)
  • Unrestricted14 hp
  • ModificationsNone permitted

How it gets built

From sketch to start line

The program runs on a one-year vehicle cycle: every season starts from a clean sheet and ends with a new car on the start line.

  1. Phase 01

    Concept & analysis

    Targets, hand calculations, load cases and design reviews with faculty, alumni and sponsors.

  2. Phase 02

    CAD & simulation

    Full-vehicle CAD and finite element analysis, plus gear and PCB design on partner software.

  3. Phase 03

    Manufacture

    Machining, welding, fabrication, wiring and assembly, done by students.

  4. Phase 04

    Test & tune

    Track days, data logging and off-road testing to find failures before competition does.

  5. Phase 05

    Compete

    Static judging, dynamic events and endurance, and the lessons go straight into the next car.
