2026–2027 season

This season's schedule

Two events: an early-season exhibition race to shake the car down, and the SAE International competition we build toward all year. How events are judged · Photo gallery.

  1. Oct 2–4, 2026 · Exhibition

    OktoBajaFest

    Seven Springs · Colton, NY

    North America's largest Baja exhibition race, with roughly 30 cars from 15 universities. Our first run in race conditions: time to dial in the drivetrain and suspension, collect data and give newer members competition experience.

  2. May 20–23, 2027 · Target event

    Baja SAE Williamsport

    Williamsport, PA

    Our target SAE International competition for 2027 (subject to the registration lottery). Four days of judging, dynamic events and the endurance race, and our benchmark against North America's best Baja programs.
