The competition

What is Baja SAE?

Baja SAE is an international collegiate design competition organized by SAE International (bajasae.net). Student teams design, manufacture and race a single-seat, all-terrain vehicle that has to withstand rough terrain, jumps, rocks and mud, and complete a multi-hour endurance race.

Every team runs the same engine (a Kohler CH440, restricted from 14 to 10 hp) under the same safety rules, so results come down to design, weight, reliability and execution, judged on the course and by engineers from industry.

02

Competition format

Judged three ways

A Baja SAE event scores the whole program, from the engineering report and cost sheet to how the car holds up after hours of abuse.

01

Static & business

Technical inspection, design judging, a cost report and a sales presentation, defended in front of industry judges.

02

Dynamic events

Acceleration, hill climb or traction, manoeuvrability and suspension events under demanding off-road conditions.

03

Endurance

A four-hour wheel-to-wheel race where reliability and pit-crew coordination count as much as outright speed.

03

The team

Who we are

Rough Rider Racing (RRR) is the University of Ottawa's Baja SAE team and one of the Faculty of Engineering's competitive design teams, with members from mechanical, electrical, software and computer engineering and other programs.

  • Design, manufacture and race a competitive Baja SAE vehicle that complies with the current rules.
  • Represent the University of Ottawa at competitions across North America and build a program that lasts for future students.
The crew

What members learn

Can't learn this in a lecture

Members graduate with a portfolio of real, tested hardware and the experience of delivering a vehicle to a fixed deadline.

01

Engineering practice

Manufacturing skills

03

Project execution

Budgeting, scheduling, risk management, communication and delivery to fixed deadlines.
