Why partner with us

Four ways in

Visibility on campus and at competition, plus direct access to the students carrying out the work.

01

Brand exposure

Logo placement on the vehicle, team apparel, paddock banner and website; recognition on Instagram and LinkedIn, including a sponsor announcement; and visibility at vehicle unveilings, campus events and competitions.

02

Talent & recruitment

Access to engineering students with demonstrated experience in cross-functional technical teams, workshop and lab environments, and long-term project delivery, including a portfolio of members and their work.

03

Technical partnership

In-kind support of any value: machining, materials, components, software, fabrication and testing. Sponsors can be recognized as the named supporter of a subsystem and work directly with the students responsible for it.

04

Community & education

Sponsorship directly funds practical engineering education in Ottawa. Sponsors are invited to technical workshops, design reviews and networking events with the wider uOttawa engineering community.

Your brand reaches uOttawa engineering students and faculty, prospective students and families, alumni, the Ottawa engineering and manufacturing community, and the teams, judges and industry reps at every competition.

Typical annual program budget

The program runs on a one-year vehicle cycle, so each season's budget designs, builds and races a brand-new car. Sponsor support lets the team make sound engineering decisions without compromising on safety or reliability.

04

Talent & engagement

Meet them before they graduate

Direct contact with team members throughout the year for recruitment, knowledge sharing and technical collaboration.

01

Information sessions

Your engineers or recruiters outline projects, technologies or career paths and meet students from several subteams.

02

Technical workshops

Practical sessions on CAD, drivetrain design, wiring standards, simulation, safety or manufacturing, delivered by your technical staff.

03

Tours & demos

View the car and discuss design decisions with the team. For higher tiers, the car can come to your site or recruitment events where logistics allow.

Opt-in

The Baja Talent Package

A portfolio of team members and the engineering work they've done on the vehicle, for employers in engineering, manufacturing, automotive, aerospace, defence, robotics and consulting. Provided to every sponsor once per semester, at all tiers. Participation is voluntary: members choose whether their information and work are shared.

  1. Profile

    You specify the profile you're after, e.g. second- and third-year mechanical students with SolidWorks, CNC and vehicle design experience.

  2. Portfolio

    We compile members matching that profile, documenting the subsystems they designed, analysed and manufactured.

  3. Delivery

    You receive resumes, CAD work and project documentation, with each member's subsystem experience, graduation year and availability.
