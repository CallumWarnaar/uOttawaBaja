Where the money goes

2026–27 budget

~$40K

Typical annual cash budget

That's the cash we spend each season to design, build and race a brand-new car.

In-kind sponsorship comes on top of it. Donated machining, materials, components and software never show up in the cash budget, but they make a massive impact on how we design and develop the car: they let us build a better car than the cash budget alone could.

84%Materials + getting to competition
10–15Members travelling per event
  • Materials & hardware54%

    Chassis tubing, suspension, drivetrain, braking and steering parts, fasteners, sensors, wiring, batteries and consumables.

  • Competition travel & lodging30%

    Travel and accommodation for 10–15 members per event, plus trailer and vehicle transport.

  • Services & contracts12%

    Professional machining and fabrication, driveshaft work, shock rebuilding and transponder subscriptions.

  • Testing & rentals2%
  • Tools & equipment2%

Materials, components, services (machining, welding, heat treatment, coating, printing, apparel) and tools count at fair market value toward a tier. Machining time and materials help most. Academic software licences are recognized separately as Software Partners.
