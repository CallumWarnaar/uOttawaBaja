The competition

What is Baja SAE?

An international collegiate design competition run by SAE International (bajasae.net). Teams design, build and race a single-seat off-road car that has to survive jumps, rocks, mud and a four-hour endurance race.

Every team runs the same Kohler CH440 (restricted from 14 to 9.5 hp) under the same rules, so design, weight and reliability decide the results.

Competition format

Judged three ways

Events score the whole program, from the cost report to how the car holds up after hours of abuse.

01

Static & business

Technical inspection, design judging, a cost report and a sales presentation, defended in front of industry judges.

02

Dynamic events

Acceleration, hill climb or traction, manoeuvrability and suspension events under demanding off-road conditions.

03

Endurance

A four-hour wheel-to-wheel race where reliability and pit-crew coordination count as much as outright speed.

The team

Who we are

Rough Rider Racing (RRR) is the University of Ottawa's Baja SAE team and one of the Faculty of Engineering's competitive design teams, with members from mechanical, electrical, software and computer engineering and other programs.

  • Design, build and race a competitive, rules-compliant Baja car.
  • Represent uOttawa across North America and leave a stronger program for the next students.
