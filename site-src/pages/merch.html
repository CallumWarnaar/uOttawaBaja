Why it matters

Fuel for the build

We build a brand-new car every season, with a typical budget of around $40K. Sponsors carry most of that, and merch helps cover the rest.

  • Every dollar of profit goes into the car: raw tubing, suspension components, machining and replacement parts.
  • It helps pay for competition registration and for getting the car, the trailer and 10–15 members to each event.
  • It helps keep the team open to any student who wants to learn, whatever their program or experience.
  • Wearing it on campus and at events shows uOttawa's Baja SAE team to the next wave of members and sponsors.
