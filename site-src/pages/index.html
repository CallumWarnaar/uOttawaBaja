Who we are

Student-built. Grit-tested.

One of the Faculty of Engineering's competitive design teams. Each season starts from a clean sheet and ends with a car built to survive jumps, rocks and mud. Students do the engineering; faculty advisors and alumni coach.

How we work

Five crews.
One car.

Four technical subteams own the car's systems. Administration keeps it funded and on the road.

01

Chassis

Frame, roll cage, ergonomics and mounting points. Stiffness, safety, weight and serviceability.

 02

Suspension

Geometry, springs and dampers, tuned for ride height, handling and durability over rough terrain.

 03

Drivetrain

CVT tuning, gearbox design, gearing, shafts, hubs and bearings, built for efficiency and reliability.

 04

Electrical

Wiring harness, kill switches, sensors and data acquisition for safe, reliable operation.

 05

Administration

Sponsorship, purchasing, budgeting, marketing, events and competition logistics.
