Sponsorship · 2026–2027

Partner with Rough Rider Racing

Support hands-on engineering education at the University of Ottawa — and get sustained visibility with future engineers and technical professionals.

Start a conversation Download the package (PDF)
Why partner with us

Visibility on campus and at competition, plus direct access to the students doing the work

1 — Brand exposure

Logo placement on the vehicle, team apparel, paddock banner and website; recognition on Instagram and LinkedIn, including a sponsor announcement; and visibility at vehicle unveilings, campus events and competitions.

2 — Talent & recruitment

Access to engineering students with demonstrated experience in cross-functional technical teams, workshop and lab environments, and long-term project delivery — including a portfolio of members and their engineering work.

3 — Technical partnership

In-kind support of any value — machining, materials, components, software, fabrication and testing. Sponsors can be recognized as the named supporter of a subsystem and work directly with the students responsible for it.

4 — Community & education

Sponsorship directly funds practical engineering education in Ottawa. Sponsors are invited to technical workshops, design reviews and networking events with the wider uOttawa engineering community.

Our audience: uOttawa engineering students and faculty, prospective students and families at recruitment events, alumni, the Ottawa engineering and manufacturing community, our social media followers, and the teams, judges and industry representatives at every competition.

Where the money goes

2026–2027 budget: $50,260

The program runs on a two-year vehicle cycle, so each season's budget funds both the car being raced and the start of its replacement. Sponsor support lets the team make sound engineering decisions without compromising on safety or reliability.

In-kind sponsorship

Materials, components, services (including machining, welding, heat treatment, coating, printing and apparel) and tools are valued at fair market value and matched to the corresponding tier. Contributions of machining time and materials are of particular value to the team. Software provided under academic licensing is recognized separately as a Software Partner.

Talent & engagement

Meet the engineers before they graduate

Direct contact with team members throughout the year for recruitment, knowledge sharing and technical collaboration.

Information sessions

Your engineers or recruiters outline projects, technologies or career paths and meet students from several subteams.

Technical workshops

Practical sessions on CAD, drivetrain design, wiring standards, simulation, safety or manufacturing, delivered by your technical staff.

Tours & vehicle demos

View the vehicle and discuss design decisions with the team. For higher tiers, the car can come to your site or recruitment events where logistics allow.

The Baja Talent Package Opt-in

A portfolio of team members and the engineering work they've completed on the vehicle, for employers in engineering, manufacturing, automotive, aerospace, defence, robotics and consulting.

  1. 1 · Profile. You specify the profile you're looking for — for example, second- and third-year mechanical students with SolidWorks, CNC and vehicle design experience.
  2. 2 · Portfolio. The team compiles members matching that profile, documenting the subsystems they designed, analysed and manufactured.
  3. 3 · Delivery. You receive resumes, CAD work and project documentation, with each member's subsystem experience, graduation year and availability.

Provided to every sponsor once per semester, at all tiers. Participation is voluntary; members choose whether their information and work are shared.

Becoming a sponsor

Let's talk

We work with organizations of all sizes, from local suppliers contributing machining time or materials to larger companies looking for recruitment access and brand visibility. We'd welcome the chance to discuss an arrangement suited to your goals.

  1. Contact the team at baja@uottawa.ca.
  2. An introductory meeting, in person or online, to discuss your objectives.
  3. Agreement on a tier and benefits, confirmed in a written sponsorship agreement.
  4. Your branding goes on the vehicle, paddock banner, team apparel and website.
